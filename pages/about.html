Our story

Started in Mbale, built for Uganda

Will's Tech Store opened in Mbale in 2023 with one goal: put authentic tech in Ugandan hands at fair prices. Today we serve customers in Kampala and Mbale, and ship across the country.

We're not just sellers, we're your tech partners. We help you choose the right gear for your budget, deliver it with care, and stay on the line after the sale for warranty claims, returns and questions.
