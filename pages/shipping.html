Delivery times start once your order is confirmed with you. Order early in the day for same-day delivery in Kampala.

Payment

  • MTN Mobile Money
  • Airtel Money
  • Cash on delivery
There is no card payment on this website. After you place an order we confirm it with you and agree how and when to pay.

Tracking

You get an order number when you check out. Use it on the Track order page with your phone number or email.

Outside Kampala and Mbale

We deliver everywhere in Uganda, by courier. The fee depends on the destination, and we confirm it with you on WhatsApp before you pay.
