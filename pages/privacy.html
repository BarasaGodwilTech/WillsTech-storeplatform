Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

We keep this simple: we collect what we need to sell you things and look after you afterwards, and nothing more. We aim to handle personal data in line with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Orders: your name, phone number, email (if given), delivery address and what you bought.
  • Account: if you create one, your name, email, phone, saved addresses, wishlist and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Messages: anything you send through the contact form, and your email if you join our newsletter.
  • On your device: your cart, theme choice and a sign-in token, kept in your browser's storage. See our Cookies page.

How we use it

  • To process, confirm and deliver your orders, and contact you about them (often on WhatsApp or by phone).
  • To provide your account and support.
  • To send newsletter emails, only if you subscribed. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Who sees it

Our team, and the service providers we use to run the store (such as our database host and, for deliveries, our riders or couriers, who only get what they need to deliver). We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers on this site.

How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed for warranty, accounting and legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Message us on WhatsApp or email willstechstoreug@gmail.com.
