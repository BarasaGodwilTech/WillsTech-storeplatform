Cookies & storage

Last updated: November 2026

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for a few things that make the store work:

WhatWhy
Your cartSo items stay in your cart between visits.
Sign-in tokenKeeps you signed in if you have an account.
Theme (light/dark)Remembers your colour preference.
Checkout detailsSaves your name, phone and address on this device to speed up your next order.
Wishlist (when signed out)Keeps the products you saved on this device. If you sign in, they move into your account.
Recently viewed, search history, compare listShows products you looked at, your recent searches and products you're comparing. Stays on your device.

You can clear all of this any time in your browser settings. If you do, you'll be signed out and your cart will be emptied.
