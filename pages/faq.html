Help & FAQ

Quick answers about ordering, delivery, payment and returns.

Ordering
Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest with just your name, phone number and delivery details. An account is optional, but it lets you track orders and save a wishlist and addresses.

How does ordering work?

Add items to your cart, check out, and you get an order number straight away. We then confirm your order and set a delivery date, and every update shows on your tracking page. You pay when it arrives.

Are your products authentic?

Yes. We source from authorised distributors and sell brand-new, sealed products.

Delivery
Where do you deliver?

We deliver everywhere in Uganda: our own riders in Kampala and Mbale, and courier to the rest of the country. For courier deliveries we set the fee on your order and show it on your tracking page before you pay anything.

How much is delivery?

Delivery cost depends on where you are. We confirm the fee with you up front, before you pay anything. Any free-delivery offers are announced on the site.

How long does it take?

Orders confirmed before 3 pm in Kampala are usually delivered the same day. Mbale is typically next day. We confirm the exact time with you.

How do I track my order?

Use the Track order page with your order number and the phone number or email you gave at checkout. Signed-in customers can also see all orders in their account.

Payment
How can I pay?

MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or cash on delivery. We tell you exactly how and when to pay when we confirm your order.

Do I pay on the website?

No. There is no card payment on this site. Placing an order reserves it, and we then confirm and arrange payment with you directly.

Can I use a discount code?

Yes. Enter it at checkout and the discount is applied to your order total before you place the order.

Returns & warranty
What if my item is faulty or not as described?

Message us within 30 days with your order number. We'll arrange a repair, replacement or refund. See the Returns & warranty page for details.

Do products come with a warranty?

Products carry the manufacturer's warranty where one applies. We tell you the warranty terms for your product when we confirm your order.

Your account
I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and we'll send you a reset link.

How is my data used?

Only to process your orders and support you. See our Privacy policy for the details.
