Returns & warranty

If something isn't right, we'll make it right.

Last updated: November 2026

If your item is faulty or not as described

Message us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a short description (a photo or video helps). We'll arrange a repair, a replacement or a refund, depending on the product and what you prefer.

What we need from you

  • The item, its accessories and original packaging where possible.
  • Your order number and the phone number or email used at checkout.
  • The item must not be damaged by misuse, water (unless it is rated for it) or unauthorised repair.

Warranty

Products carry the manufacturer's warranty where one applies. We tell you the warranty terms for your product when we confirm your order. Warranty claims are handled through us, so you don't have to chase the manufacturer.

Refunds

Approved refunds are paid back the way you paid (mobile money or cash). We'll tell you how long it takes when we approve the refund.

Changed your mind?

Tell us as early as you can, ideally before your order is dispatched. Once a sealed item has been delivered, return it unopened and in resale condition and we'll do our best to help.

The quickest way to start a return is to contact us with your order number.
